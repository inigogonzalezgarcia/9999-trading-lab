# Data specification

CSV and Parquet require `timestamp, open, high, low, close, volume`. V1 execution also requires a nonnegative finite `spread_pips` value on every entry/exit candle; trades are blocked if it is absent. Timestamps must include a timezone or an explicit IANA timezone setting on import. Mixed aware and naive values are rejected. Naive local timestamps on DST gaps/folds are rejected rather than guessed. Internally timestamps are UTC candle-close times; `config/data.yaml` records whether source timestamps denote bar open or close. Duplicate normalized timestamps and invalid/non-finite OHLCV rows are rejected. Out-of-order rows are reported and sorted. Missing intervals and weekend-dated bars are reported, never filled. Dataset identity is a SHA-256 of source bytes.

Resampling is UTC-aligned, right-closed with OHLC first/max/min/last and volume sum; labels are bar close time. The close timestamp is the earliest availability time for a completed candle. Causal consumers must filter to timestamps at or before the decision instant. No synthetic data is added.
